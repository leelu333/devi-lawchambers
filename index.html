The Chambers

Real estate counsel, shaped by a decade inside the industry.

Before founding the chambers, Adv. Leelu Devi M spent a decade in litigation and at the head of the legal function of leading Bengaluru developers. She has seen land deals from the developer’s desk: title, conversion, joint development, approvals, funding and launch.

That experience shapes how the chambers work. Title is examined as a lender or a purchaser’s counsel would examine it. Joint development and sale documents are drafted with the later stages in mind: conversion, Khatha, RERA registration and bank funding. Where disputes arise, they are managed with the commercial picture in view.

The chambers act for landowners, developers, purchasers and businesses, and also take up civil, family and criminal matters for individual clients.

“A clean title is the foundation of every project. Most disputes can be prevented at the diligence stage.” Adv. Leelu Devi M, Founder

From Land to Launch

Developers usually hand a project from one lawyer to the next. At the chambers, the same advocate who reads the first RTC stays on the file until the project is launched.

  1. Title & diligenceRTCs, mutation registers, encumbrances, litigation searches and a written title report.

Our People

Adv. Leelu Devi M

Founder & Principal Advocate

Leelu Devi M is the Founder and Principal Advocate of Devi Law Chambers. She is a real estate and corporate lawyer with more than a decade of experience advising developers, corporates, landowners and lenders across Karnataka.

Leelu is recognised by her clients for her command of land law and title. She has led the legal function of several of Bengaluru’s established real estate developers, including Mantri Developers, Garudachala Estates, Royale Developers and Sri Sumeru Realty, and advises Saroj Groups as Legal Head on land procurement across the State. In these roles she has guided residential and commercial developments from land acquisition and title clearance through joint development, statutory approvals, RERA registration and project funding to launch.

Her title work is extensive: on average, she has conducted due diligence on over 500 acres of land for each developer she has advised. She structures and negotiates joint development agreements, advises boards and senior management on legal risk, and manages arbitration and litigation arising from land and development transactions. She began her career in litigation at George & Co. Advocates.

She founded Devi Law Chambers to offer corporates, builders, landowners and individuals the same rigour she brought to large developers: complete diligence, precise documentation and practical, commercially minded advice.
